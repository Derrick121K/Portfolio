THE IDEA BEHIND THE HOUSE — 02

A South African point of view. A world of appreciation.

The African Wood House is envisioned as an independent luxury South African art brand, bringing handcrafted wooden artwork and the people behind it into focus.

Our ambition is to create a destination where visitors can discover the character of each piece, appreciate its making and connect with a distinctive expression of African craft.
