05 / Working approach

Good software starts with clear thinking.

I value clear requirements, maintainable implementation, transparent progress and responsible handover.

01

Understand the problem. Agree on users, business goals, scope, constraints and success criteria.

02

Design and build in steps. Break work into reviewable milestones and test important workflows.

03

Communicate clearly. Share progress, technical decisions, risks and blockers early.

04

Handover responsibly. Document deliverables and protect customer data, credentials and private source code.
